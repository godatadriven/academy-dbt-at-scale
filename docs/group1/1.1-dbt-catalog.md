# dbt Catalog

Over the next few topics you're going to refactor modeling patterns, close testing gaps, and remove repeated logic across three domains in `mediapulse_base` you probably haven't touched yet: `podcasts`, `ads`, and `news`. Before any of that starts, get oriented using dbt Catalog - it's how you'll navigate a project you didn't build, and how you'll spot problems by tracing lineage and metadata instead of reading every model's SQL cold.

**Prerequisite:** a production (or staging) deployment environment for `mediapulse_base`, with at least one successful `dbt build` job run, so Catalog has metadata to display.

**Goal:** use Catalog to find, ahead of time, the specific gaps and questions the next three topics will have you fix - so you're not discovering them cold when you get there.

## 1. Find the biggest gaps

Open Catalog's **Recommendations** tab for `mediapulse_base`, then look at the sidebar resource counts (Model / Source / Test / Seed, etc.). Cross-reference what Catalog flags against what you find by reading a bit of the YAML yourself. At minimum, look for:

- a model that's doing real transformation work but has no description on it at all - Catalog's documentation coverage view will surface this faster than clicking through every model file
- any staging model whose columns have no tests at all - not just missing `unique`, missing *everything*
- a source with zero downstream models, or a model that looks disconnected from any source in the lineage graph at all (worth asking: how would a model even end up with no source upstream of it?)

You don't need to fix any of these now. **Write a ranked list: which three would you raise with your own team first, and why those three over the others you found?**

??? tip "Hint: where recommendations come from"
      Catalog's recommendations largely mirror the checks in the open-source `dbt_project_evaluator` package, so the same reasoning you'd apply to that package's output applies here. See the [dbt Catalog FAQs](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/explore/dbt-explorer-faqs).

## 2. Trace a domain you haven't touched yet: podcasts

Filter Catalog's project view by **Modeling layer = Staging**, and find `stg_podcasts__shows` and `stg_podcasts__listens`.

- Check how each is materialized. Compare that against `dim_shows` further downstream - is it materialized the same way, or differently? You don't need to know *why* yet, just note the difference.
- Open `stg_podcasts__listens` in the lineage graph. Does it pull from one source, or two? A staging model that joins more than one source together upstream of everything else is often a sign the join belongs in a later layer instead.

??? tip "Hint: materializations"
      See the [materializations docs](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/materializations) for the trade-offs between `view`, `table`, `incremental`, and `ephemeral` - useful context for why staging and marts are often materialized differently.

## 3. Column-level lineage on the news domain

Find `stg_news__articles` and click into its `category` column. Trace it downstream: where does `category` ultimately end up, and does it get renamed or transformed anywhere along the way?

??? tip "Hint: column-level lineage is an Enterprise feature"
      If you don't see per-column lineage, check your plan tier - it's only available on Enterprise and Enterprise+ plans. See the [dbt Catalog FAQs](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/explore/dbt-explorer-faqs) for the full feature-by-plan breakdown.

## 4. Read the ads domain like a stranger would

Open `stg_ads__campaigns`, `stg_ads__impressions`, and `stg_ads__spend` in Catalog. Look at test coverage across all three - roughly how much of this domain has *any* column-level test at all?

Then open `stg_ads__campaigns` specifically and look at its lineage node. Does it show a `Source` feeding into it the way `stg_podcasts__shows` did in step 2 - or does the graph make it look like the model has no upstream at all? If something looks off here, make a note of it rather than digging into the SQL yet.

**Write down:** your ranked list of gaps from step 1, what you noticed about materializations and joins in the podcasts domain, where `category` ends up downstream, and what looked unusual about `stg_ads__campaigns`'s place in the lineage graph.

**Deliverable:** your ranked list of gaps (1), your podcasts domain notes (2), your answer on `category`'s downstream path (3), and your notes on the ads domain's test coverage and lineage oddity (4).
