# Jinja/Macros & Custom Schema Logic

*Group 2 - Part 1*

A deeper look at one specific, real macro in this project: `mediapulse_platform`'s custom `generate_schema_name` override, and what it means that this override doesn't automatically apply to `mediapulse_analytics` too.

## Exercise

**Goal:** understand exactly why this override exists, and check whether the newer analytics-side seeds actually need one.

1. **Read the macro in full**
   Open `mediapulse_platform/macros/generate_schema_name.sql`. Identify the two branches: one for seeds under `_seeds_setup`, one for everything else.

   ??? tip "Hint: The two behaviors"
       For `_seeds_setup` seeds, a custom schema name is used exactly as given (no prefix). For everything else, dbt's normal `{{ target_schema }}_{{ custom_schema_name }}` pattern applies. See [Custom schemas](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/custom-schemas) for how `generate_schema_name` is invoked and what context it has access to.

2. **Explain the "why"**
   Why does `mediapulse_platform` need seed schemas to land *exactly* as configured (e.g. `news`, `podcasts`, `ads`, `streamview_legacy`) rather than getting a target-schema prefix? What would go wrong for the project's `source()` definitions if seeds landed in a prefixed schema instead?

3. **Check the analytics side**
   Open `mediapulse_analytics/seeds/_seeds_setup/_seeds_setup.yml` (the CRM seeds). `mediapulse_analytics` has no `generate_schema_name` override of its own. Would these CRM seeds land in the schema their `source()` definitions expect, or would they get a prefix? Reason it through from dbt's default macro behavior.

**Deliverable:** your answers to steps 2 and 3, written as if explaining the design decision to a new team member.

## Extension

dbt Mesh doesn't share macros across projects the way it shares public models. Reason through what that actually means in practice.

1. **The constraint**
   `generate_schema_name` is a project-level override - each dbt project gets exactly one, and it is not inherited from an upstream project through a `dependencies.yml` relationship.

   ??? tip "Hint: Macro dispatch and packages"
       If you want to genuinely share macro logic across projects (not just override behavior locally), the relevant dbt concepts are packages and [macro dispatch](https://docs.getdbt.com/reference/dbt-jinja-functions/dispatch) - not dbt Mesh's model-level `ref()`/`access` system, which only governs models.

2. **Work through the scenario**
   Suppose `mediapulse_analytics` later needs the exact same seed-schema behavior `mediapulse_platform` has. Would you: (a) duplicate the macro into `mediapulse_analytics/macros/`, (b) extract it into a small internal package both projects install via `packages.yml`, or (c) something else? Argue for one option.

3. **Generalize the lesson**
   Beyond schema naming, name one other kind of project-level configuration (hint: think about what else lives in `dbt_project.yml` alongside `models:` and is not covered by `access:`) that a dbt Mesh setup does *not* automatically share between projects, and explain why that matters for teams maintaining multiple mesh projects.

**Deliverable:** your recommendation from step 2 with reasoning, plus your answer to step 3.
